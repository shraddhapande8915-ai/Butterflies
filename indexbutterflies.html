BUTTERFLIES

Butterflies are flying insects of the order Lepidoptera with scaly wings and a complete metamorphosis life cycle.Butterflies are colorful, flying insects belonging to the order Lepidoptera. They go through a complete physical change called metamorphosis, which includes four stages: egg, caterpillar, chrysalis (pupa), and adult. An adult butterfly has a body divided into three parts (head, thorax, and abdomen), six legs, two antennae, and four wings covered in tiny, delicate scales. Instead of a mouth for chewing, they have a long, tube-like straw called a proboscis to sip flower nectar and liquids. You can find butterflies all over the world except in Antarctica, and they play a vital role in pollinating plants and flowers.
